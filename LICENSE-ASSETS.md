- Images, textures, models and store art: `itch/`, `ui/logo.png` and any `.png`, `.jpg`, `.glb`, `.gltf`, `.blend` files.
- Narrative and design texts: `docs/HISTORIA.md`, `docs/CUTSCENE_ABERTURA.md`, `docs/GDD.md`, and the in-game dialogue, radio calls and notes.

Everything else (source code, shaders, scenes, the procedural audio generator in `audio/`) is MIT; see `LICENSE`.
